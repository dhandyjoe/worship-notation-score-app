How to edit this score

Editing happens right on the score — no dragging needed. Here's everything you can do:

  • 🎛️ Two writing modes — when you start a new song you pick a mode (shown as a badge next to the title). 🎸 Chords is for chord progressions like C, Am7, G/B with live chord suggestions and transpose. 🔢 Numbers is for Nashville number notation (1–7 with high/low octaves) and enables a lyrics field under each beat. The editor adapts its suggestions and controls to the mode you chose.
  • ＋ Add a chord — click any empty beat, then type a chord (e.g. C, Am7, G/B). Pick from the live suggestions or press Enter.
  • ✏️ Edit or replace — click a beat that already has a chord to retype it. Clear the field and confirm to remove it.
  • 🖱️ Split the rhythm — right-click a beat (or long-press on touch) to split it into ½, ⅓ or ¼ beats for faster chord changes.
  • 🎵 Slash chords — type the bass after a slash, like D/F#, and it's placed as one chord.
  • 🔤 Rename titles & sections — click the song title, artist, key or a section name to edit it inline.
  • ➕ Add sections — use + Add section below the score to append a new part (Verse, Chorus, Bridge…).
  • 🎚️ Transpose — use the key ▲ / ▼ controls to shift every chord up or down a semitone.
  • ↩️ Undo & Redo — made a mistake? Use the Undo / Redo buttons below the key info, or press ⌘Z / Ctrl+Z to undo and ⌘⇧Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z to redo. Up to 50 steps are remembered.
  • ⧉ Copy & paste a bar — hover a bar and click the copy icon (⧉) to grab all its chords, then click paste (⎘) on another bar to drop them in. Great for repeating chord patterns quickly.
  • 🎼 Add multiple bars at once — use + Add bar under a section to extend it. Each new bar follows the section's time signature automatically.
  • 🔗 Share a song — from My Songs, click Copy Link (🔗) on any song to copy a share link. Send it to a friend — when they click Attach Link and paste it, the song imports straight into their library.
